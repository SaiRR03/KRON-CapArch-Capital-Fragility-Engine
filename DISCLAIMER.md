## Research Alpha

KRON Capital Fragility Engine™ v1.0 Alpha is an experimental, deterministic research tool. It is published for evaluation, testing, reproducibility and critique.

It does not provide investment, financial, legal, accounting, tax, credit-rating or regulatory advice. Outputs must not be treated as a recommendation to invest, lend, restructure, proceed, delay or reject a project.

## Model limitations

The public Alpha does not estimate:

- probability of default;
- empirical probability of boundary exceedance;
- loss given default;
- portfolio diversification or correlation;
- contagion;
- empirical reference-class probabilities;
- a universal Capital Fragility Index.

Stress Capacity Used is a deterministic distance-to-boundary measure, not a probability.

All financial thresholds in the bundled synthetic example are illustrative user inputs. They are not represented as universal market standards or proprietary KRON commercial decision rules.
